# Graphics sources

The Science and Math diagrams are original parameterized drawings. They are decorative scientific illustrations, not measurements or physical simulations. Their source is in `src/diagrams.ts`.

The twelve animal engravings come from *The Swiss Family Robinson*, George Routledge and Sons, 1891, via Wikimedia Commons. The archive identifies each as public domain in the United States and notes that status can differ elsewhere. These historical images retain that status; the Impression Personal Use License does not claim ownership of them or impose new restrictions on them.

Original image bytes are bundled unchanged. Six files use size variants generated by the archive. In the app, an SVG luminance filter removes the scan's paper and gives the lines the current ink color. The scans are embedded before rasterization, so PNG exports include the engravings and preserve background transparency.
